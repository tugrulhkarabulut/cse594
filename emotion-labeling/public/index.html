Labeling emotion in short messages

You'll read 5 short messages from a public dataset of social media posts and choose the single emotion the writer is expressing. Takes about two minutes.

How to choose

  • Label what the writer feels, not how the message makes you feel.
  • Pick the best single fit, even when a message is ambiguous. Go with your instinct.
  • The dataset's authors stripped punctuation, capitalization, and emoji, so the text reads a little flat. Judge it as-is.

Recorded with your labels and the time you spend on each message. Nothing else is collected.
